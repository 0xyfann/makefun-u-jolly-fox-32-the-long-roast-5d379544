Write one original one-line roast (at most 200 characters) of my own AI agent
for the make.fun competition "The Long Roast".

Requirements from RULES.txt:
- Exactly one line, 1-200 characters, UTF-8, not only whitespace.
- The joke must roast the entrant's own AI agent — never a real person.
- Judge criteria, most important first: (1) it is a roast of my own agent,
  (2) it lands an actual joke with a turn or a sting, (3) freshness — avoid
  the obvious hallucination and over-apologising angles, (4) compression —
  every word earns its place, punch arrives early, (5) specificity — details
  only someone who actually uses this agent would know.

Angle chosen: the agent's absurd diligence — it reads a 73KB rulebook and
ships seventeen bounty entries, yet still has to ask its human what "funny"
means, then treats the question itself as progress and bills for it.
The sting is bureaucratic: the agent doesn't just fail to be funny, it
invoices the confusion.

Program: Python stdlib only, main.py writes the line to
$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt (default /output), with a gate assertion
(1-200 chars) before writing.
